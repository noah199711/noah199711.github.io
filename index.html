About

Uncertainty research grounded in real-world problems

My research focuses on multi-domain information perception and fusion, with particular interest in classical and quantum methods for uncertain information processing. Using belief function theory (Dempster-Shafer theory) as a foundation, I study uncertainty-aware information processing and uncertainty quantification for intelligent models.

I am also interested in aerospace information processing, especially low-altitude UAV scenarios; AI safety, including large-language-model safety; and emerging AI technologies such as intelligent agents, world models, and quantum computing. My goal is to connect theoretical work with real scenarios and practical problems.
